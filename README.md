This Power BI report provides interactive visualizations to monitor, analyze, and gain insights into business seasonality and trends. <br/>It allows stakeholders to track sales performance across regions, branches, and business categories, enabling data-driven decisions.

## Business Requirement

The report addresses the need to identify trends in sales across different business categories and regions. <br/>It helps in understanding seasonal patterns, high-performing periods, and underperforming areas to optimize strategies and resource allocation.

## What It Solves

**Real-Time Insights**: Visibility into sales trends by region, branch, and category.<br/>
**Trend Analysis**: Month-wise comparison of average sales vs overall sales<br/>
**Seasonal Patterns**: Identifies peak and low sales periods.<br/>
**Efficiency**: Reduces manual effort in analyzing sales from multiple sources.<br/>

## Data Sources

**Google Sheets:** Operational and historical sales data.<br/>
**SQL Server Database:** Transactional and detailed sales records.<br/>

## Key Features

**Dynamic Filters & Slicers**: Filter by region, branch, loan ID, employee, purpose, and sub-purpose.<br/>
**Trend Visualization**: Area chart showing average sales vs overall sales.<br/>
**Category Comparison:** Analyze sales across different business categories and sub-categories.<br/>
**Interactive Dashboard:** Drill-down into months, branches, or categories for granular analysis.<br/>
**Decision Support:** Highlights trends and patterns to facilitate business decisions.<br/>

## Tools & Technologies

**Power BI Desktop**: Report development and visualization.<br/>
**DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Custom calculations and measures.<br/>
**Power Query (M)**: Data transformation and cleansing.<br/>

## How to Use

Open the Power BI file (.pbix) in Power BI Desktop.<br/>
Connect to the required data sources (Google Sheets, SQL Server, etc.).<br/>
Apply filters and slicers to explore trends by region, branch, purpose, and sub-purpose.<br/>
Drill down into specific months or categories to analyze detailed performance.<br/>
Export visuals or share the report via Power BI Service for collaboration.<br/>
