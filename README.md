This Power BI report provides interactive visualizations to monitor, analyze, and gain insights into business seasonality and trends. It allows stakeholders to track sales performance across regions, branches, and business categories, enabling data-driven decisions.

##Business Requirement

The report addresses the need to identify trends in sales across different business categories and regions. It helps in understanding seasonal patterns, high-performing periods, and underperforming areas to optimize strategies and resource allocation.

##What It Solves

Real-Time Insights: Visibility into sales trends by region, branch, and category.
Trend Analysis: Month-wise comparison of average sales vs overall sales
Seasonal Patterns: Identifies peak and low sales periods.
Efficiency: Reduces manual effort in analyzing sales from multiple sources.

##Data Sources

Google Sheets: Operational and historical sales data.
SQL Server Database: Transactional and detailed sales records.
Excel/CSV Files (Optional): Supplementary datasets.

##Key Features

Dynamic Filters & Slicers: Filter by region, branch, loan ID, employee, purpose, and sub-purpose.
Trend Visualization: Area chart showing average sales vs overall sales.
Category Comparison: Analyze sales across different business categories and sub-categories.
Interactive Dashboard: Drill-down into months, branches, or categories for granular analysis.
Decision Support: Highlights trends and patterns to facilitate business decisions.

##Tools & Technologies

Power BI Desktop: Report development and visualization.
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Custom calculations and measures.
Power Query (M): Data transformation and cleansing.
Optional: Python/R scripts for advanced analytics or automation.

##How to Use

Open the Power BI file (.pbix) in Power BI Desktop.
Connect to the required data sources (Google Sheets, SQL Server, etc.).
Apply filters and slicers to explore trends by region, branch, purpose, and sub-purpose.
Drill down into specific months or categories to analyze detailed performance.
Export visuals or share the report via Power BI Service for collaboration.
